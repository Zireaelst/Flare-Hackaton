# Landing assets

Two things here are borrowed and should be replaced before submission.

**Videos and the card illustration** are hotlinked from a CloudFront bucket
belonging to whoever generated them. They resolve today and carry no expiry
signature, but they are neither ours nor licensed to us, and the bandwidth is
someone else's. Every one of them sits over a solid background colour, so the
layout is correct whether or not they load.

**The font** is Manrope standing in for TT Norms Pro, which is a commercial
licence. If the licence is bought, drop the woff2 files here and swap the family
in `globals.css` — nothing else needs to change.

Replace the hero video by dropping `cta-background.mp4` in this folder; the
closing section prefers a local file over any remote source.
